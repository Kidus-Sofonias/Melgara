## Positioning Statement

> **Melgara: the East Africa–to–world gateway in minerals.** For industrial buyers and commodity traders who stake production on grade, we deliver 25 years of mining expertise, in-house processing, and mine-to-port logistics — verified, transparent, and fluent across continents.

**The promise in one line:** *"We don't promise ore. We deliver the exact grade, on spec, on time."*

**Tagline shortlist:**
1. *"From the Horn of Africa to the world's foundries."*
2. *"Mined. Verified. Delivered."*
3. *"Where Africa's minerals meet the world."*
4. *"The earth's wealth, engineered for delivery."*

## Brand Personality — "The Reliable Titan"

- Voice: measured, expert, understated power. Every claim specific (grades, percentages, countries) — never vague.
- Tone: grounded · rigorous · global · transparent · quietly dominant
- Never: "Committed to Quality" filler, stock corporate clichés, hype

## Story Pyramid

1. **Proven since 2000** — a quarter-century of trading raw materials
2. **Verified quality** — grades backed by full chemical analyses & spec PDFs
3. **Mine-to-port, fully owned** — extraction, processing, logistics under one roof
4. **A bridge, not a broker** — real presence across 7 countries + Dubai, 4+ languages
5. **Built for heavy industry** — steelworks, foundries, cement, sugar refineries worldwide

## Audience Map

| Tier | Who | Needs | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Procurement directors / raw-material buyers (EU/Asia) | Verified grades, analyses, delivery, compliance | Request a quote / spec download |
| Secondary | Commodity traders & merchant houses (Dubai, Singapore) | Availability, volumes, grades, terms | Supply inquiry |
| Tertiary | East African manufacturers, logistics partners, investors | Reliability, footprint, capacity | Contact / partnership |

4. **Live stats ticker** — tonnage, grades, countries served
5. **Transparency Hub** — documentation, compliance, quality process
6. **Smart quote engine** — product · volume · destination lead capture
7. **EN + አማርኛ** from day one
